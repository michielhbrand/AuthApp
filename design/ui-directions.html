Five directions. Light and dark, side by side.

The same login and dashboard content is rendered through five distinct standards, each with a complete light and dark token set. Compare palette, typography, shape, density, and tone without changing the underlying product.

At a glance

Palette and personality

Each card shows the light and dark palette side by side: L for light, D for dark. One dominant accent carries each direction in both modes; the neutrals and surface depth change underneath it.

Decision support

How to choose

All five directions share a 4 px spacing rhythm, one dominant accent, restrained elevation, and AA-targeted text contrast in both modes. The choice is about the product voice: calm utility, operational precision, human warmth, future-facing atmosphere, or engineering rigor.

Direction Personality Choose when Watch out for

Each direction is now a complete dual-mode standard. Once one is selected, the next step is to encode its light and dark palettes as Vuetify theme tokens: colors, typography, radii, elevation, focus states, and component defaults.
